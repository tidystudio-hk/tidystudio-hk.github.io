Summary

The site looks good and the content is clear, but the home page is heavy and slow to appear on a phone. Most of the delay comes from two causes: one very large hero image and scripts that load before the page is allowed to draw.

On mobile, the product comparison table forces sideways scrolling. For search, most pages are missing descriptions and several share the same title. None of this needs a redesign. The four high-priority fixes take about four hours and address the biggest problems.

Findings, in order of priority

Hero image is 2.9 MB

SpeedHigh
Why it matters
It is the first thing visitors wait for. On a phone connection the page stays mostly blank until this one file arrives.
The fix
Export it at the size it is displayed (1600 px wide is enough), convert to WebP with a JPEG fallback, and serve a smaller version to phones with srcset.
Effort
30 minutes

Six scripts block the page from drawing

SpeedHigh
Why it matters
The browser must download and run the chat widget, two analytics tags and a slider library before it shows any content.
The fix
Add defer to scripts that are not needed for first paint, load the chat widget after the page is idle, and remove the slider library (it is used on one page only).
Effort
1 hour

Product comparison table overflows on phones

MobileHigh
Why it matters
The whole page scrolls sideways on screens narrower than 720 px, which hides the "Add to cart" column on the pages where people decide to buy.
The fix
Wrap the table in a scrollable container and switch to stacked cards below 600 px, keeping price and the buy button visible.
Effort
1 hour

14 of 18 pages have no meta description

SEOHigh
Why it matters
Without one, search engines pick their own snippet, often a cookie notice or menu text, so the listing gives people little reason to click.
The fix
Write a specific one- or two-sentence description for each page (up to about 155 characters), starting with the product and category pages.
Effort
1.5 hours

Content jumps while the page loads

SpeedMedium
Why it matters
Images load without reserved space, pushing text and buttons down as they appear. Visitors lose their place or tap the wrong thing.
The fix
Add width and height attributes to all images and reserve a fixed height for the promo banner.
Effort
45 minutes

Five pages share the same title

SEOMedium
Why it matters
All five category pages are titled "Shop | Harbor & Pine". Search engines and browser tabs cannot tell them apart.
The fix
Give each page a unique title in the form "Category name | Harbor & Pine Furniture", under about 60 characters.
Effort
45 minutes

Menu and footer links are too small to tap

MobileMedium
Why it matters
Several links are 24 px tall and packed together, so thumbs regularly hit the neighbouring link.
The fix
Increase tap targets to at least 44 px in height and add spacing between footer links.
Effort
30 minutes

Images, styles and scripts are not cached

SpeedMedium
Why it matters
Returning visitors download every file again on every page, even though the files have not changed.
The fix
Set long-lived Cache-Control headers for static files in the hosting settings.
Effort
30 minutes

23 images have no alt text

SEOLow
Why it matters
Screen-reader users hear nothing useful for these images, and search engines have no text to understand the product photos.
The fix
Add a short, factual description to each product photo; mark purely decorative images with an empty alt="".
Effort
1 hour

No sitemap is published

SEOLow
Why it matters
A sitemap helps search engines discover every page, including new products that are not yet linked from the menu.
The fix
Generate sitemap.xml, reference it in robots.txt and submit it in Google Search Console.
Effort
30 minutes
