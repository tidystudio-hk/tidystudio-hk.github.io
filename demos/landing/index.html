Our day

We start while the street is still asleep.

Good bread cannot be hurried. Our dough is mixed by hand the afternoon before, rests overnight in the cool room and goes into a stone-deck oven before sunrise.

We bake in small batches and stop when the flour for the day is used up. Some mornings that means the croissants are gone by ten. We would rather sell out than sell yesterday's.
