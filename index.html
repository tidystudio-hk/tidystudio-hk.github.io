FAQ

Short answers.

How fast is delivery?

Most jobs are delivered within 24–72 hours of the quote being accepted. The delivery date is written in your quote, so you know before you commit.

What if I want changes?

One free revision is included with every job. Tell us what is not right and it gets adjusted. Work beyond the agreed scope is quoted separately, before it starts.

How do I pay?

Through a secure payment link sent with your quote. You never need to share card details by email.

What does "from" mean in the prices?

It is the price of a typical small job of that kind. After seeing your details we send one exact, fixed price. It does not change unless the scope does.

What do you need from me?

A link to the site, sheet or project, a short description of what you want, and access only to what the job requires. For automations, that means the accounts being connected.

Tell us what needs doing.

You will get a fixed quote and a delivery date by email. A useful first message includes:

  • A link to your site, sheet or project
  • What is wrong, or what you want built
  • Any deadline you are working to
